# ScanWise Health

Build a premium, responsive, India-first radiology test discovery and appointment-assistance platform named “BookMyScan.in”.

Primary market: Delhi NCR at launch. Future expansion: PAN India.

Core positioning:
“Find the Right Scan. Choose the Right Centre.”

BookMyScan.in helps patients discover radiology tests, understand preparation requirements, compare suitable diagnostic centres, upload prescriptions, and request appointment support. It also includes specialised sections for PET imaging, nuclear medicine, PSMA PET-CT and Lu‑177 PSMA therapy navigation.

Important medical and compliance rules:
- This is not a self-diagnosis or treatment recommendation platform.
- Add clear medical disclaimers across the website.
- Use “Always consult your treating doctor” where appropriate.
- Lu‑177 PSMA therapy eligibility and treatment decisions must be stated as determined only by qualified treating specialists and hospitals.
- Do not use guaranteed outcomes, cure claims, misleading discounts or unsupported medical claims.

Design direction:
Premium, modern, medically credible, patient-friendly and conversion focused.
Use deep navy blue, white, cool grey and a restrained teal accent colour.
Avoid discount/coupon website styling.
Use polished typography, clean cards, subtle shadows, professional medical imagery, excellent spacing and accessible contrast.
Mobile-first responsive design is mandatory.

Main navigation:
BookMyScan.in | Scan Tests | Scan Centres | Advanced Cancer Imaging | Lu‑177 PSMA Therapy | Scan Cost Guide | Book a Scan
