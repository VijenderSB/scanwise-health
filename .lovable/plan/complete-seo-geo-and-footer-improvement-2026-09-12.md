# Complete SEO, GEO and footer improvement

## Goal
Make SavoScan.com easier to understand and discover in search engines and AI answer systems, while strengthening Delhi NCR relevance and improving the footer without changing working booking, pricing, lead-capture or medical-safety flows.

## Search and AI discovery
- Audit every public content page and give it a unique, concise title, description, Open Graph title/description/type, Twitter card, self-referencing canonical URL and page URL.
- Keep staff, sign-in and booking-confirmation pages out of search results while leaving public booking and information pages discoverable.
- Add accurate structured data where supported: organization/website information, local service coverage, breadcrumbs, scan/service pages, medical informational pages and visible FAQs.
- Use only claims already supported by the site. Do not invent ratings, reviews, prices, partner verification, addresses or medical outcomes.
- Strengthen India and Delhi NCR signals in natural page copy and metadata, including Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad and Greater Noida where relevant.
- Improve crawlability and internal linking across scan types, cities, preparation guides, advanced imaging and booking pages.
- Preserve the current crawler-friendly robots rules. Because no published public URL exists yet, defer a new sitemap until publication provides the correct domain URL.

## Footer redesign
- Expand the footer into clear sections for popular scans, Delhi NCR locations, patient resources and assistance.
- Add direct links for MRI, MRCP, CT, PET-CT, ultrasound, mammography and other high-intent pages.
- Add the verified WhatsApp contact, stronger booking action, service-area wording and the exact SavoScan.com tagline.
- Keep the emergency, medical-advice, pricing and privacy-conscious assistance disclaimers prominent and readable.
- Improve spacing, hierarchy and mobile stacking while preserving the navy, white and teal identity.

## Technical details
- Use TanStack route `head()` metadata and JSON-LD scripts; do not add a second head-management system.
- Keep canonical and `og:url` values route-specific and relative until a public domain is connected.
- Add `staticData.sitemap` decisions only if the existing router sitemap generator is present; otherwise do not invent a sitemap implementation.
- Verify metadata in rendered HTML, structured-data validity, internal links, desktop/mobile footer layout, no overlap, no horizontal overflow and clean runtime/build diagnostics.
- Run the SEO foundations scan again after the corrections and mark only fully corrected findings as fixed.

## Scope preserved
- No route removals, pricing changes, new medical claims, fake local offices, or changes to centre/search/booking functionality.
