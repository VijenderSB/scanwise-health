# Roman Hinglish Heading Update

## Goal
Update important headings across the public SavoScan website to friendly Roman Hinglish while keeping the existing design, functionality, medical accuracy, and search visibility intact.

## What will change
- Convert prominent public H1 and H2 headings into concise Roman Hinglish.
- Update important section labels, conversion banners, and major call-to-action headings where Hinglish improves clarity.
- Cover the homepage, scan discovery and detail pages, centre and locality pages, preparation guides, comparison and cost pages, booking flow, nuclear medicine therapy pages, contact/support pages, and legal pages.
- Keep the brand lockup, scan and therapy names, city names, clinical terminology, prices, form fields, detailed medical copy, safety notices, and legal wording in clear English where translation could create ambiguity.
- Use a consistent tone, for example:
  - “Your Scan. Your Choice. Bigger Savings.” → “Apna Scan Chunein. Zyada Savings Paayein.”
  - “Big Savings on Popular Scans” → “Popular Scans par Badi Savings”
  - “Request a Personalised Treatment Estimate” → “Personalised Treatment Estimate Mangwayein”
  - “How patient assistance works” → “Patient Assistance Kaise Kaam Karti Hai”

## Search and medical safeguards
- Preserve English scan and therapy keywords in every medically important heading, such as MRI, MRCP, CT, PET-CT, Nuclear Medicine Therapy, and Lu-177 PSMA Therapy.
- Keep page titles, descriptions, canonical links, structured data, and clinical/legal body text accurate and searchable; only adjust metadata where a mixed English/Hinglish title remains clear.
- Do not translate medical terms into informal wording that could imply diagnosis, treatment suitability, guaranteed savings, or confirmed availability.

## Verification
- Check all public page families for one clear H1 and readable heading hierarchy.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts for wrapping, overlap, and navigation stability.
- Confirm booking, WhatsApp, enquiry forms, redirects, scan search, and therapy links remain unchanged.
- Run the existing build and browser checks before completion.
